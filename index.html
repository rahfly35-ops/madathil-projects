The project

Electric Vehicle Charging for Council Fleets (EVCCF)

A full delivery plan for a Victorian Government program to install 120 EV charging stations across 57 council sites, part of the broader Destination Charging Across Victoria initiative. Built as a Master's capstone (Deakin University, MIS798) and graded High Distinction. Below, the project-management work it took to plan it. Hover any card for detail.

Mandate hover ›

Zero-emission fleets

Ready 26 councils to run and maintain EV fleets, cut public-sector emissions and seed a second-hand EV market.

Delivered across four geographic zones: Western/South-Western, Eastern/South-Eastern, Central/Northern Victoria, and Metropolitan Melbourne. Success meant councils able to operate and maintain EV fleets, local job creation, and alignment to Victoria's net-zero-by-2050 target with interim cuts of 28 to 33% by 2025.
Structure hover ›

Balanced matrix, 4 zones

A Director of Project Management over four zones, with zonal PMs sharing authority with functional directors.

Dual reporting across Engineering, IT, Operations, Compliance, Finance and HR. Matrix conflict was managed up front with defined roles and escalation paths; the Director of Project Management held cohesion and consistent standards across all zones and 57 sites.
Approach hover ›

Plan · cost · de-risk

Feasibility, a Level-3 WBS, an MS Project schedule, phase-wise budgeting, and a phased risk register.

SWOT and PESTLE feasibility, a Statement of Work with AS/NZS acceptance criteria, a WBS to Level 3, an MS Project schedule with critical path and slack, a phase-wise budget to $1,800,155, and an 18-risk register using a four-quadrant response framework (Avoidance / Mitigation / Transfer / Acceptance).
